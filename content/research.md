# Research

## Common ownership and institutional disclosure (Quant Kiosk)

A replication of Backus, Conlon & Sinkinson's (2019) common-ownership κ index, built on QuantKiosk 13F holdings data (2013–present) after the original paper's WRDS/Compustat data sources proved structurally inaccessible. A separate, related thread studies AUM dynamics around 13F amendment filings — funds that file amendments show a real, statistically robust spike in reported AUM growth in the amendment quarter itself, surviving fund-specific trend controls, a formal pre-trend test, and a placebo check.

## Crowd belief compression in a live forecasting competition (JTC / SportsPredict)

A full statistical forecasting pipeline (point-in-time Elo, a Poisson/Dixon-Coles goal model, an ordered-logit outcome model) built for a scored World Cup prediction tournament. The central empirical finding: the platform's crowd consensus is structurally compressed toward 50% relative to a calibrated estimate — a relationship that held essentially unchanged when the sample size doubled. Four independent tests (a recalibration check, a learned blending model under two validation schemes, a linear regression, and a closed-form cost argument) all rejected a machine-learned blend of model and crowd in favor of the hand-built probabilistic pipeline, grounded in small-sample model-selection theory.

## Independent quantitative research

Self-directed work spanning cryptocurrency volatility prediction (a real, correctly-scoped result separating predictable volatility magnitude from near-random direction), tail-risk estimation, neural-network option-hedging benchmarked against Black-Scholes, and a Kalman-filter adaptive pairs-trading strategy.

## Prediction market infrastructure

Trading system design for Kalshi and Polymarket prediction markets — data ingestion, market-making logic, and backtesting infrastructure.

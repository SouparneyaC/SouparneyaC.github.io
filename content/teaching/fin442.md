# FIN442 — Teaching Assistant

> "I would like to recommend Souparneya Chakrabarti who is my current TA," the professor's own email to the department, written the same day he asked whether I wanted to continue into the spring.

## Overview

TA for [FIN442, International Corporate Financial Management](/coursework/fin442/), Fall 2025, under Professor Tor-Erik Bakke, the same course taken as a student the previous summer. The role is confirmed directly by an email chain: Bakke wrote to the department recommending me as his current TA, and the department's own staffing coordinator followed up the same day asking whether I wanted to continue as a TA in the spring, since FIN442 itself isn't taught then.

## What I actually did

Case material from the TA period survives, dated into October and November 2025, squarely inside the semester I was TAing rather than the summer I took the course myself: a writeup on AES's case, explaining why the devaluation of South American currencies hurt a company earning revenue in local currencies but holding dollar-denominated debt, and a writeup on Nextel Peru's competitive position, working through NII Holdings' declining EBITDA and rising debt load as the reason its Latin American subsidiaries eventually had to be sold. Whether these specific documents were reference solutions I prepared or submissions I reviewed isn't confirmed from the files alone; what's confirmed is that they exist, dated correctly, in a course folder that otherwise holds my own coursework from a different semester entirely.

## Where this shows up elsewhere

Bakke's recommendation is the direct reason the department asked which other Finance courses I'd be a good fit for, which led to [Course Assistant work for FIN330 and FIN570](/teaching/fin330-fin570/) the following semester.
